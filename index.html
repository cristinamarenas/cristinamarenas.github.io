About

How I think about social.

I start with the strategy and work out to the post. Every piece of content should have a reason to exist, an audience that cares, and a way to tell if it worked.

Then I measure it and report it plainly, so leadership sees what moved and what to do next. I have done this for national brands, sports media, and a public school district, and I build the calendars, approvals, and reports that keep the work running when the trend is over.
